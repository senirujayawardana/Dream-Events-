CLEAR FROM THE START

Booking terms.

A short guide to how event enquiries become confirmed bookings with Dream Events.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. An enquiry is not a booking

Submitting a website form or sending an email is an enquiry only; it does not confirm a booking. Our team will review your details, follow up to discuss availability and requirements, and provide a written quote if we can support your event.

2. Quotes, deposits, and payment

Package prices shown on the website are starting points; the final scope and price depend on the event details and will be set out in a written quote or agreement. Deposit amounts, payment dates, and any payment schedule are agreed case by case and provided in writing before payment.

The written agreement will explain how the booking is confirmed. Submitting an enquiry or receiving a follow-up message alone does not confirm it.

3. Cancellation, rescheduling, and refunds

Cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms are set out in the written agreement for each event. Please review those terms before paying a deposit. If anything is unclear, contact us for clarification before payment; the written agreement governs the specific booking.

4. Contact and privacy

We handle enquiry details as described in our Privacy Policy. For questions about an enquiry or proposed booking, email dreammakerofc@gmail.com.
