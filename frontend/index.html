08 A few helpful answers

before we begin.

Have another question? Send us an inquiry and our team will be happy to help.

Talk to Dream Events ↗
What kinds of events do you organize?

We plan concerts and festivals, corporate experiences, weddings, and personal celebrations. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll discuss the right approach.

Can a package be tailored to my event?

Yes. Our packages are starting points. Share your guest count, priorities, and event details so our team can discuss a suitable plan.

How do I reserve a package?

Choose a package, submit your contact details and event dates, and our team will follow up to discuss availability and next steps. A request is not a confirmed booking.

Where do you organize events?

Dream Events is based in Sri Lanka’s Southern Province. Include your preferred location in your inquiry and we’ll let you know whether we can support it.

How can my organization discuss a partnership?

Use the partnership inquiry form on our Investor Relations page to share your organization and goals. Our team will review your message and get back to you.
