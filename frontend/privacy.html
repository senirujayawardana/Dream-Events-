YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

This page explains how Dream Events handles information you share through our event, package, and partnership enquiries.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Information we collect

We collect the details you choose to provide in our forms or by email. This may include:

  • Event or package enquiries: your name, email, phone number, event type or package, dates, approximate guest count, preferred location, and comments.
  • Partnership enquiries: your name, work email, phone number, organization, and message.
  • Technical and anti-spam information needed to host the website and verify form submissions.

How we use your information

We use enquiry details to respond to you, discuss event requirements and availability, prepare a quote, and manage an event or partnership if you choose to proceed. We also use technical and anti-spam information to operate and protect the website.

Who may process or receive it

Form submissions are stored using Supabase and email notifications are delivered using Resend. The website is hosted by Vercel and forms use Cloudflare Turnstile for spam prevention. These service providers process information as needed to provide their services.

If you proceed with an event, we may share relevant details with venues or event suppliers where needed to plan and deliver the services discussed with you. Service providers may process or store information outside Sri Lanka depending on their systems and configuration.

Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, manage an event or partnership, maintain appropriate records, and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of information you provided. We may need to verify your identity and may retain some records where required or permitted.

Security and updates

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as our services or practices change; the date above shows the latest update.

For privacy questions or requests, email dreammakerofc@gmail.com.
