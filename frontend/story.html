OUR STORY

The Dream Events Story.

Roots in Collaboration,
Built for the Extraordinary

01 WHERE IT BEGAN

Roots in collaboration,
built for the extraordinary.

Every great movement starts with a spark. For us, that spark ignited in the halls of St. Thomas’ College, where the three of us served on the Board of Prefects. Long before Dream Events officially came to life in 2024, we were the ones behind the scenes—coordinating school productions, rallying crowds, and discovering the absolute magic that happens when an idea turns into a shared live experience.

What began as a shared passion for leadership and production quickly evolved into something much bigger: a commitment to redefining how Sri Lanka experiences live entertainment.

02 WHAT WE DO

We don’t just coordinate timelines.
We build worlds.

Headquartered in the vibrant Southern Province, Dream Events is a full-scale event production and artist management powerhouse. We don’t just coordinate timelines; we build worlds. From electrifying musical concerts and cultural festivals to high-profile corporate gatherings, we treat every single project as a blank canvas waiting for a masterpiece.

03 MOMENTS THAT BROUGHT US TOGETHER

A few milestones
close to our hearts.

3,000+music lovers at Mihiraviye
900+attendees at Colours of Love
2024the year Dream Events began

Our journey so far has been defined by milestones we are immensely proud of. We brought the island to life with “Mihiraviye,” our signature lantern concert festival that drew over 3,000 passionate music lovers together under a sky of lights, featuring legendary acts like Hot Chocolate, Shihan Mihiranga, Dilu Beats, Raveen Tharuka, Bhashi Devanga, Tehan Perera, and Dasun Madushan. We’ve painted the Southern Province in vibrant colors with our “Colours of Love” Holi Festival (engaging 900+ attendees), and continuously elevate corporate standards through flawless execution.

04 WHAT COMES NEXT

We are just
getting started.

While our roots remain deeply planted in the South, our vision spans island-wide expansion. As we grow, we are stepping into the world of bespoke wedding planning—bringing our signature high-energy production, seamless logistics, and creative vision to life for couples ready for an unforgettable celebration.

At Dream Events, we dream it, we build it, and we bring people together.
